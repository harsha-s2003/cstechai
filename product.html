CORE CAPABILITIES

Built to identify, monitor and analyse change

A focused set of capabilities that helps teams detect meaningful changes, compare geospatial conditions and turn imagery into actionable intelligence.

Automated Change Detection

Detect visible changes across time-series imagery using rule-based and analytical workflows.

Multi-Temporal Image Comparison

Compare imagery from different dates to identify land, asset or environmental changes.

Feature-Level Analysis

Track changes in specific features such as buildings, roads, vegetation, water bodies or site boundaries.

Geospatial Visualization

View detected changes clearly through map-based outputs, overlays and intuitive geospatial layers.

Alert & Monitoring Workflows

Support periodic monitoring and exception-based tracking for faster operational response.

Decision-Ready Outputs

Generate clear outputs that support planning, reporting, verification and field action.
